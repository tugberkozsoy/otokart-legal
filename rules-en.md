# Otokart — Community Rules

**Last updated:** 21.11.2026

## 1. What Otokart is

On Otokart you photograph real vehicles you see on the street, collect their cards and share them with other car enthusiasts. To keep the community safe and enjoyable for everyone, you must follow these rules. By joining the community you accept them.

## 2. What you can share

Only photos of real vehicles that you took yourself. Screens, posters, toys, models or AI-generated images are not allowed.

Plates and faces are blurred automatically; photos with readable plates or clear faces are not published. Still, check your photo before sharing.

## 3. What is prohibited

Nudity and sexual content; violence, blood or pointing weapons; hate speech, discrimination and hate symbols; harassment, threats and bullying; usernames or images containing insults or profanity.

Other people's personal information (plates, faces, phone numbers, addresses); advertising and spam; impersonating a person or brand; cheating in points or rankings; photos taken while driving or in dangerous situations.

## 4. Comments, likes and follows

Comments, card captions and profile bios are public and checked automatically before publication. You may share your own social media accounts (such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or VSCO) in your bio, captions and comments. Phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, school names, age and other details that identify you or someone else, and other people's accounts, may not be shared. Opinions, criticism and jokes about cars are welcome; insulting, mocking, harassing, threatening or persistently bothering people is prohibited.

Advertising, selling, links other than social media profiles (websites, shops, group or channel invites), promoting your own account in comments on other people's cards, and engagement spam such as 'follow for follow' or mass liking are prohibited. Spreading your invite code in comments and captions, or inflating invites with fake accounts, is also prohibited; invite badges may be revoked.

A caption can have up to 5 #hashtags; hashtags are visible to everyone in search and follow the same rules. Hashtags that target a person, carry personal information, or are misleading or irrelevant (used only to stand out) are not allowed; hashtags that break the rules are disabled.

Card owners can delete comments on their own cards and turn comments off. A comment reported by three different people is hidden without waiting for review. There is no private messaging in the app; do not ask anyone for personal information. Be careful before giving your phone number, address or school to people you meet in another app.

## 5. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Every shared photo is checked automatically before publication. Content that breaks the rules is removed; accounts of users who violate the rules may be suspended or closed without warning.

## 6. Reporting and blocking

If you see an inappropriate photo or user, report it from the ••• menu on the photo or profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; photos reported by several people are hidden without waiting for review; if the review finds them within the rules, they become visible again.

You can block a user from the same menu: you will not see each other's profiles or photos. You can unblock under Profile > Settings > Blocked users.

If your photo, comment or bio is hidden under the rules, you are told why and can appeal from the notification in the app within 30 days. If your photo was unfairly rejected when sharing, you can write to [İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI].

## 7. Your photos

You retain the rights to the photos you share. By sharing, you allow Otokart to show them to other users in the app according to your photo setting. When you turn sharing off or delete your account, your photos are deleted from the server.

## 8. Age and contact

You must be at least 13 years old to use Otokart.
