# Otokart — Privacy Policy and KVKK Notice

**Last updated:** 21.11.2026

## 1. Data controller

This document is Otokart's privacy policy and the information notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The data controller for personal data processed in the Otokart app is Mehmet Tuğberk Özsoy.

Contact: [iletisim@otokart.info](mailto:iletisim@otokart.info)

## 2. What we process

Account: the app-specific user identifier Apple provides when you sign in with Apple. Your name and email address are not requested or collected.

Community profile: the username you choose, an optional province you select, your avatar (icon and color, or a profile photo), your optional bio (a short introduction of up to 150 characters) and the cards you place in your showcase.

Cards: the make, model and trim of the recognized vehicle, its rarity, points, date saved, verification status, and the record of who found a vehicle first on Otokart.

Recognition photos: the photo you take or select to recognize a vehicle is sent to our server and to an AI service provider after license plates and faces are automatically blurred on your phone. It is not stored on the server after recognition.

Shared photos: the photo of cards you captured with the camera and that were verified (with plates and faces blurred) is stored on the server to be shown in Discover and on your profile, according to your photo setting. Every photo is automatically checked before publication for being a real vehicle photo, inappropriate content, readable plates and clear faces.

Profile photo: if you add a profile photo, it is stored on the server and shown to other users after passing an inappropriate-content check.

Community interactions: the people you follow and who follow you, your likes, the posts you save (only you can see your list; the card owner is not notified), the comments and card captions you write (including their #hashtags), in-app notifications generated from these interactions, users you block, reports you make and reports about you. The app has no private messaging between users; all interactions take place in public, moderated areas. Invites: your invite code, how many people joined with it and who invited you. When you enter a code you start following the person who invited you, and they are notified of the follow.

Push notifications: if you allowed notifications and joined the community, the notification token Apple issues for this app and device, used only to notify your phone about events concerning you (new followers, likes, comments). Notifications do not include the text of comments. You can turn notification types off under Profile > Settings > Notifications.

Card issue reports: when you report a mistake on one of your cards, the issue type you choose, the description and correction you write, the card's vehicle details (make, model, generation, version, specifications, rarity), the recognition candidates and the app version. Your photo is not sent; if the card was shared publicly, only the shared photo's link key is included. Reports are read only by the Otokart team and used only to correct recognition and vehicle details.

Security and traffic records: the time, result and volume of recognition and sharing requests are kept to enforce daily limits and prevent abuse. For photo and profile photo shares, the time of sharing, account identifier and IP address are recorded to meet obligations under Turkish Law No. 5651. The server infrastructure may keep technical connection logs such as IP addresses for a limited time.

Otokart does not collect special categories of personal data (health, biometric data, etc.). Faces in photos are not recognized or matched; they are only detected in order to be blurred.

## 3. Data that stays on your phone

The location where a card was captured, the original photos in your garage, your notification preferences and reminders are stored only on your phone and are not sent to the server. Location is used to show your cards on your map and is not shared with other users. Backing up data stored on your phone is your responsibility; deleting the app deletes this data. Widgets read your streak, weekly theme and mission summary from the app's storage on your phone; they do not connect to the server. Photos taken with the lock screen camera wait only on your phone until you turn them into cards, and are deleted if not turned into a card within 7 days.

## 4. Purposes

Recognizing the vehicle in a photo and creating its card; providing community features such as points, leaderboards, friends, showcase and Discover; preventing cheating, abuse and inappropriate content; keeping your account and the service secure; reviewing reports and card issue reports, and correcting recognition and vehicle details; meeting legal obligations.

## 5. Collection method and legal bases

Data is collected electronically and automatically from the information you enter and the photos you take while using the app.

Data is processed under KVKK Art. 5/2-c to provide the app (formation and performance of a contract), Art. 5/2-f for security and preventing cheating and abuse (legitimate interest), Art. 5/2-ç to comply with legal obligations (including retention of traffic information under Law No. 5651), and Art. 5/2-e to establish, exercise or defend a right.

Photo sharing and profile photos are your choice: you can turn photo sharing off at any time under Profile > Settings > Who can see my photos, and remove your profile photo.

Sharing a card image or story image in other apps such as Instagram or WhatsApp is your choice: the image is sent to the app you pick in the share menu and is subject to that app's terms. The story image contains your card, username and invite code; it contains no location or city.

## 6. Automated checks

Photos to be shared, profile photos, comments and card captions are checked automatically with AI; comments and captions also pass a word filter. Content that does not pass is not published. These checks can make mistakes. If you believe your content was rejected unfairly, you can object by writing to [iletisim@otokart.info](mailto:iletisim@otokart.info); your objection will be reviewed by a person.

## 7. Who we share data with

Supabase: database, authentication and file storage infrastructure. Servers are located in Germany (Frankfurt, European Union).

Anthropic: AI vehicle recognition and checks on photos, comments, card captions and bios to be published. The service is provided in the United States. Only blurred photos and the text being checked (with the card's make/model) are sent to this service; your username and Apple identifier are not.

Apple: Sign in with Apple and delivery of push notifications (Apple Push Notification service).

Transfers abroad are made in accordance with the conditions of KVKK Art. 9.

Other users: your username, province, avatar, bio, points, cards (excluding location), showcase, card captions and their #hashtags (hashtags and your cards' make/model are also searchable), comments and, according to your photo setting, your shared photos are visible in the app. Only you can see your follower and following counts and lists, the like counts on your cards and the posts you save.

Competent authorities: only when legally required and only to the extent necessary.

Your personal data is not sold and is not used for advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account under Profile > Settings > Delete my account, your profile, card records on the server, shared photos, profile photo, friendships and records linked to your account are permanently deleted, and Otokart's Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked at Apple. When you turn sharing off, your photos on the server are deleted.

Retention periods: recognition security records 90 days; in-app notifications 90 days; daily quota counters and recognition candidates 30 days; reports, card issue reports, hiding reasons and appeals 1 year; moderation decision records 13 months; comments, likes, saved posts, follows, invite records, bio and card captions (with their hashtags) until you delete them or delete your account; if a card you saved is deleted, the save is deleted too; the notification token until you sign out or delete your account (automatically when Apple reports the app was removed). Traffic information for shares (photos, profile photos, comments and captions: time, account identifier, IP address; not the text itself) is kept for 1 year as required by Law No. 5651 and deleted automatically after 13 months at the latest. Because of this legal obligation, traffic information is kept for this period even after the account is deleted.

Where a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires it, the relevant records may be kept only for that period.

## 9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS); server access is restricted by authorization rules and users can only modify their own data. Shared photos are served through unguessable links; anyone who has such a link can view the photo. When you turn photo sharing off, your photos on the server are deleted. Nevertheless, no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. In the event of a data breach, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board and affected individuals will be notified as required by KVKK.

## 10. Age limit

Otokart is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from people under that age. Users under 18 should use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. If you become aware that someone under 13 has created an account, notify [iletisim@otokart.info](mailto:iletisim@otokart.info) and the account will be deleted.

## 11. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data and, where conditions are met, deletion or destruction, and notification of these actions to recipients; to object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can send requests to [iletisim@otokart.info](mailto:iletisim@otokart.info). Requests are concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. We may need to verify your identity (for example, by asking for your username in the app). You can also delete your account and data from within the app. If your request is rejected or you find the answer insufficient, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.

## 12. Changes

This document is updated as new features are added. Material changes are announced in the app and, where necessary, your consent is requested again. Last updated: 21.11.2026.
